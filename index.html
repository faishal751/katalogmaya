About Us

A Premium Trading Integrator from Bandung, Indonesia

PT Maya Ananda Lestari is an export management and trading company headquartered in Bandung, West Java. We operate as an integrator between Indonesia's best local producers and international buyers, taking responsibility for the full path from origin to port.

We source directly from trusted local producers, applying rigorous selection and verification so that every shipment reflects the quality our partners expect.

End-to-End Global Supply Chain

Sourcing, consolidation, documentation and shipment coordination handled under one accountable partner.

Strict Quality Control Compliance

Specification checks and food-safety compliance applied before products are cleared for export.

Reliable Cross-Border Trade Execution

Clear communication, accurate paperwork and disciplined delivery schedules for every contract.
